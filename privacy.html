Privacy Policy

MenuCat · Last updated October 31, 2026

MenuCat doesn't collect any data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Nothing about you or your Mac is ever sent anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

MenuCat saves a few settings on your Mac so your cat is the same next time: its name, coat, accessory, mood, and the options you pick in the menu. These never leave your Mac, and deleting the app deletes them.

Window positions

So your cat can sit on top of your windows, MenuCat checks where app windows are on screen. It only reads their size and position, never their titles or what's in them. Nothing is recorded or shared.

Purchases

If you adopt a second cat, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. MenuCat never sees your payment details.

Children

MenuCat collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
