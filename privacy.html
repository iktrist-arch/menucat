Privacy Policy

MenuPets (formerly MenuCat) · Last updated November 1, 2026

MenuPets doesn't collect any data. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Nothing about you or your Mac is ever sent anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

MenuPets saves a few settings on your Mac so your pets are the same next time: their animal, name, coat, accessory, mood, and the options you pick in the menu. These never leave your Mac, and deleting the app deletes them.

Window positions

So your pets can sit on top of your windows, MenuPets checks where app windows are on screen. It only reads their size and position, never their titles or what's in them. Nothing is recorded or shared.

Purchases

If you adopt more pets or unlock pack animals, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. MenuPets never sees your payment details.

Children

MenuPets collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
