MenuPets Support

Little pixel pets that live on your Mac's windows: a cat, dog, fox, bunny, turtle, bird or hamster, plus the Country Animals and Safari packs. (Formerly MenuCat.)

Where's my pet?

Click the MenuPets icon in the menu bar. Each of your pets has a line there; hover over it for its animal, coat, accessory, name and toy. Activity and Settings are in the same menu.

Can I change which animal I have?

Yes. Choose Animal in your pet's menu. The cat, dog, fox, bunny, turtle, bird and hamster come with the app, each with up to ten coats.

What are the animal packs?

Country Animals: Bald Eagle (USA), Dragon (China), Kangaroo (Australia), Beaver (Canada), Tiger (India), Snow Monkey (Japan) and Toucan (Brazil). Safari: Lion, Elephant, Giraffe, Zebra and Rhino. Each pack animal is a one-time purchase, unlocked on its own, and comes with its own coats and toy. Once it's unlocked, any of your pets can be one.

Toys and treats

Choose Give a Toy in your pet's menu. Every animal has its own: a yarn ball for a cat, a bone for a dog, a wheel for a hamster, a carrot for a bunny, and more. Drag a toy somewhere and your pet goes after it.

It doesn't sit on my windows

Make sure Settings → Sit on Top of Windows is ticked in the menu. Your pet only sits on windows that are on the same display as it.

I have more than one display

Choose Move to Next Display in your pet's menu to send your pet to another screen. With more than one pet, each can live on a different display.

My pet looks sad

It misses you! Hover your cursor over it to pet it, and it'll cheer up. To change how often it misses you, choose Settings → Pets Miss Me.

Open MenuPets when I log in

Tick Settings → Start at Login in the menu. You can also turn it on or off in System Settings → General → Login Items.

How do I get more pets?

Choose Adopt Another Pet… and pick any animal. Each extra pet is a one-time purchase, and you can have up to five at once. You can send a pet home for now and bring it back anytime.

I adopted pets on another Mac

Choose Adopt Another Pet… and then Restore Purchases. You won't be charged again. Your pet spots and any pack animals come back. If you adopted the second cat in MenuCat, it's still yours.
