MenuCat Support

A little pixel cat that lives on your Mac's windows.

Where's my cat?

Click the cat in your menu bar. Everything about your cat is in that menu: its name, coat, accessory, and what it should do.

It doesn't sit on my windows

Make sure Sit on Top of Windows is ticked in the menu. Your cat only sits on windows that are on the same display as it.

I have more than one display

Choose Move to Next Display in the menu to send your cat to another screen. With two cats, each can live on a different display.

My cat looks sad

It misses you! Hover your cursor over it to pet it, and it'll cheer up.

Open MenuCat when I log in

Tick Start at Login in the menu. You can also turn it on or off in System Settings → General → Login Items.

I adopted a second cat on another Mac

Choose Adopt Another Cat… and then Restore Purchase. You won't be charged again.
